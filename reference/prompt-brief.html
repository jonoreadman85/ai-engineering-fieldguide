REFERENCE · PROMPTING

The prompt brief

A flexible checklist for getting useful answers without overcomplicating simple requests.

Goal: What outcome or decision do I need?

Context: What background, audience, source material, or constraints matter?

Task: What should the AI do now?

Output: What form should the answer take?

Quality bar: What would make the answer useful and correct?

For engineering tasks

Include the relevant system, users, constraints, and intended decision. Ask the AI to expose assumptions and identify missing facts that could change its answer.

For writing

Name the audience, purpose, tone, format, source material, and what the reader should do after reading. Say whether the AI should draft, edit, summarize, or critique.

For research

Define the question, scope, timeframe, preferred source types, citations, and how to separate evidence from inference.

For learning

Say what you already know, what you want to be able to do, how much time you have, and ask for a short explanation followed by practice and feedback.

Glossary

Quality bar
Observable conditions that tell you whether the answer is good enough to use.
Iteration
A focused follow-up that helps improve an answer by naming what to add, remove, or change.
Context
Background information that changes what a good answer should say.
