INTERMEDIATE · 5 MIN

Prompt for grounded research

For research, asking for an answer is not enough. Specify scope and sources, and ask the AI to show what evidence supports each important claim.

The idea

Make research verifiable by stating the question, time boundaries, source preferences, citations, and how to handle uncertainty.

See the pattern

“What are the best approaches?” gives the AI a broad, subjective target. “Compare approaches published since 2023, prioritize official documentation and peer-reviewed work, cite key claims, and separate evidence from your inference” makes the task reviewable.

Research

Question: [what you need to learn]
Scope: Focus on information published since [date].
Sources: Prioritize primary sources and official documentation.
Output: Summarize the main findings, cite the source for each key claim, and list disagreements.
Uncertainty: Separate evidence from inference and flag gaps.

How to use it

When recency matters, specify a date range or say to search current sources. Ask for links near claims, include limitations, and invite the AI to say when evidence is insufficient rather than filling gaps. Then open the sources that matter most.

TRY IT · 2 MINUTES

Put it into practice

Draft a prompt to research a question you genuinely have. Include scope, a source preference, citations, and an instruction for handling uncertainty.
