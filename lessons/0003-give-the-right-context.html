FOUNDATIONS · 5 MIN

Give the AI the right context

When an answer feels generic, the missing ingredient may be relevant context. Learn to include what changes the answer and label source material so the AI can tell instructions from evidence.

The idea

Separate task instructions from information the AI should use. Add only context that matters to the goal, audience, or constraints.

See the pattern

Imagine asking for a project plan without saying the deadline, team size, or current state. The AI must guess. Supplying a short “Context” section gives it useful facts; labeling pasted notes as source material helps distinguish them from your request.

Research and planning

Goal: Recommend the next milestone for this project.
Context: [team size, current status, deadline, constraints]
Source notes: [paste notes here]
Task: Recommend one milestone and explain the trade-off.
Quality: Use only facts in the notes for claims about our project; list unknowns separately.

How to use it

Context can include audience, current situation, examples of internal terms, constraints, and documents. For long source material, identify which parts matter and what to do if the material doesn’t answer the question.

TRY IT · 2 MINUTES

Put it into practice

Write a prompt for an AI to recommend a next step on a real task. Include context and clearly label any notes or source text you provide.
