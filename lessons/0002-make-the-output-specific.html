FOUNDATIONS · 4 MIN

Make the output specific

A clear request can still produce the wrong shape. Specify the form, length, audience, and level of detail when those affect whether you can use the answer.

The idea

Use output constraints to make an answer easier to act on. Avoid stacking arbitrary rules; each constraint should serve the task.

See the pattern

Suppose you need a team update. “Summarize this” could produce anything from three bullets to a long essay. “Write a 150-word update for engineers with a one-line decision, three actions with owners, and open questions” tells the AI what usable means.

Writing

Task: Turn these notes into an update.
Output: Under 150 words, for the engineering team. Use headings: Decision, Next steps, Open questions. Keep a direct, neutral tone.
Quality bar: Preserve the facts; mark missing owners as “TBD.”

How to use it

Pick the constraints that matter: audience, format, length, tone, and required sections. If you don’t care about one, leave it open. Overconstraining can make a response stiff or force invented details.

TRY IT · 2 MINUTES

Put it into practice

Rewrite “Summarize this for me” as a prompt with an audience, a useful output format, and one quality check.
